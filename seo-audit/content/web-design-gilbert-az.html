Contractors and trades

A large share of the Gilbert businesses searching for web design are contractors — remodelers, roofers, HVAC, landscaping, pool builders. The requirements are specific enough to be worth naming:

  • The work is the sales pitch. A gallery of finished projects, organised by type of job, does more than any amount of copy about quality and integrity. Every competitor claims those.
  • Almost all of the traffic is mobile. Someone standing in their kitchen deciding to remodel it is holding a phone. If the number is not tappable and the gallery does not load quickly on cellular, the job goes to whoever's site did.
  • Service area needs to be explicit. Customers want to know you cover them before they call. A plain list of the towns you work in prevents wasted calls in both directions.
  • Licence and bond numbers belong on the site. ROC number visible, insurance stated. It costs nothing and it removes a reason to hesitate.
  • Reviews need to be on the page. Not a link to a review site that sends the visitor away mid-decision.

None of this is exotic, and yet most contractor sites we look at are missing three or four of them.
