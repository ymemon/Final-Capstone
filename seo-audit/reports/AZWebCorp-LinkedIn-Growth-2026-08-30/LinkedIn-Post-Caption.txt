SEO progress is not one flashy percentage. It is several useful signals moving together.

During August 23–29, AZ Web Corp recorded:

• 68 active users — up 172%
• 114 sessions — up 171%
• 337 page views — up 237%
• 892 events — up 209.7%
• 14 Organic Search sessions — up 600%
• 27 referral sessions — up 440%

Across the full monthly view, Google Organic increased from 13 to 27 sessions, while EverythingIT.ie generated 22 referral sessions through our live partner link.

The starting numbers are still modest, so this is not a victory lap. The real test is whether qualified organic traffic continues growing—and whether those visits become enquiries. But users, engagement, search and referrals all improving together is a promising direction.
