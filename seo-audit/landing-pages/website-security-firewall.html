Website Security & Firewall for Arizona Businesses

A hacked site is not just a technical problem. Google blocklists infected sites, which removes you from search results entirely and shows a red warning screen to anyone who tries to visit. Recovery takes far longer than prevention.

What a web application firewall does

  • Blocks attacks before they reach your site
  • Scans continuously for malware
  • Removes infections and cleans compromised files
  • Prevents Google blocklisting and the warnings that follow
  • Mitigates DDoS traffic floods

Why WordPress sites are targeted

WordPress powers a large share of the web, which makes its plugins a standing target for automated attacks. Most breaches are not personal: bots scan continuously for known plugin vulnerabilities. A firewall blocks those attempts before an outdated plugin becomes an entry point. If you're on managed WordPress hosting, core and plugin updates are handled for you, which closes the most common route in.

Layering security properly

A firewall stops attacks, SSL encrypts traffic, and backups get you back online if something still goes wrong. They solve different problems and most sites need all three.

FAQ

My site is small. Is it really a target?

Attacks are automated and indiscriminate. Bots scan for vulnerable software, not for businesses worth attacking, so site size is irrelevant.

Does a firewall slow my site down?

No. Traffic is filtered at the CDN edge, and the caching that comes with it usually makes sites faster, not slower.
