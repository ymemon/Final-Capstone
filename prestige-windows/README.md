# Prestige Windows — Digital Growth Project

Separate client project from `../seo-audit` (which is AZ Web Corp's own site). This
folder is for **Prestige Windows**, a Mesa, AZ-based owner-operated window & door
contractor and authorized dealer for Pella, Renewal by Andersen, and American
Windows & Doors.

## Engagement

Two prior/current phases of work exist for this client:

1. **Website build** (Mar 16 – May 15, 2026, completed) — see `Prestige_Windows_Website_Blueprint.docx`
   in Downloads for the full 10-page WordPress site spec (Home, About, Products,
   Configurator, Gallery, Suppliers, Blog, FAQ, Testimonials, Contact). Site is live.
2. **Six-Month Digital Growth Roadmap** (starts Aug 2026, this is the active engagement) —
   local SEO, technical SEO, paid search, social media, reputation, referral
   partnerships. See `ROADMAP.md` for the condensed working reference, sourced from
   `Prestige windows 6 months road map..pdf` in Downloads.

## Structure

- `ROADMAP.md` — condensed 6-month engagement plan (hours, priorities, month-by-month, KPIs)
- `audit/` — technical/SEO audit findings for the live site
- `content/` — service/location pages, blog content, case studies
- `redirects/` — any redirect map work

- `social/` — content calendar, drafted posts
- `ads/` — Google Ads campaign notes, landing pages

## Scope Guardrails (from the roadmap)

- **120 hours total, 20/month, 6 months.** No automatic overage — anything beyond
  120 hours needs written client approval.
- Priority order when hours are tight: (1) tracking/conversion/technical issues,
  (2) core service + priority city pages, on-page SEO, GBP, (3) paid-search
  landing pages tied to lead quality, (4) content/case studies/social, (5)
  secondary location pages, backlinks, experimental channels.
- Do not create thin or duplicative location pages — Tier 3 markets only get
  activated when search demand and remaining hours justify it.
- Ad spend, call-tracking subscriptions, photography/videography, and other
  vendor costs are separate from AZ Web's labor hours.
