# Six-Month Digital Growth Roadmap — Prestige Windows

Source: `Prestige windows 6 months road map..pdf` (Downloads), signed by Yasir Memon
(AZ Web Corp) Aug 10, 2026. Condensed here as the working reference.

## Engagement basics

- **Client:** Prestige Windows — Mesa, AZ-based owner-operated window/door
  contractor; authorized dealer for Pella, Renewal by Andersen, American Windows
  & Doors. Boutique, direct-to-owner positioning (not lowest-price).
- **Term:** 6 months, 20 hrs/month, 120 hrs total, hard cap — no automatic
  overage. Overage requires written client approval.
- **Live site:** prestigewindowsaz.com (built Mar–May 2026, separate blueprint doc)
- **Showroom:** Prestige Home Studio LLC — prestigehomestudiollc.web.broadlume.com

## Priority markets

- **Tier 1:** Scottsdale, North Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Biltmore, Arcadia,
  Silverleaf, DC Ranch, McCormick Ranch, Gainey Ranch, Troon, Desert Mountain,
  Pinnacle Peak.
- **Tier 2:** Gilbert, Chandler, Queen Creek, Eastmark, Las Sendas, Red Mountain
  Ranch, Northeast Mesa, South Tempe, Ahwatukee, Morrison Ranch, Agritopia,
  Seville, Ocotillo.
- **Tier 3:** activate only when search demand / project history / remaining
  hours justify it.

## Priority order when hours run tight

1. Tracking, conversion paths, technical issues affecting lead gen.
2. Core service pages, priority city pages, on-page SEO, GBP, local fundamentals.
3. Campaign landing pages, paid-search optimization tied to lead quality.
4. High-value content, case studies, review systems, social (genuine assets only).
5. Secondary location pages, broader backlink outreach, experimental channels.

## Month-by-month (20 hrs each)

**Month 1 — Foundation, Website Prep & Measurement**
Discovery/positioning (2h) · Technical SEO foundation: crawl/repair, homepage,
nav, CTAs, sitemap, metadata, mobile (7h) · GA4/Search Console/Tag Manager/
call-form-text tracking (5h) · GBP setup (3h) · Social channel setup + templates
(2h) · Baseline report (1h).

**Month 2 — Local SEO Launch & Initial Lead Gen**
Core service/location page SEO — highest priority pages only (7h) · GBP
complete: services, categories, service areas, photos, review link, posting
cadence (3h) · Google Ads + landing pages: Scottsdale/PV, East Valley, brand
(4h) · Organic social content (3h) · Lead workflow/conversion refinement (2h) ·
Reporting (1h).

**Month 3 — Premium Content & Local Authority**
Expand priority location/service content: Scottsdale, PV, Biltmore/Arcadia,
Chandler, Queen Creek, premium products (7h) · Case studies + AZ homeowner
education (4h) · On-page SEO/internal linking/schema (2h) · Social: project
proof, before/after, video if available (3h) · Ads/GBP refinement (2h) ·
Reporting: impressions, calls, forms, city performance (2h).

**Month 4 — Conversion Optimization & Reputation Growth**
CRO: estimate forms, call/text CTAs, photo upload, trust signals, FAQ, mobile
(5h) · SEO content updates on underperforming pages (4h) · Review/reputation
system (3h) · Paid-search retargeting refinement (2h) · Social (4h) ·
Reporting: consultation/estimate rate by source (2h).

**Month 5 — Partnerships, Backlinks & Premium Referrals**
Referral outreach lists: builders, architects, designers, luxury RE, remodelers
(5h) · Local authority/backlinks: manufacturer, contractor, chamber, directory
(4h) · SEO/content expansion — only what data justifies (3h) · Referral
collateral + email outreach (2h) · Social (4h) · Reporting: referral inquiries,
branded search, link quality (2h).

**Month 6 — Performance Review & Controlled Growth**
Technical/organic review: positions 4-20, opportunity areas, internal links
(4h) · Content refresh on best-potential pages (4h) · Ads/conversion review by
city/device/term/landing page (3h) · Social: best-of-term proof (3h) ·
Executive 6-month report + attribution (4h) · Months 7-12 strategy (2h).

## KPIs to track

- **SEO/Local:** organic impressions/clicks, keyword visibility by city, indexed
  pages, GBP interactions, organic calls/estimate requests, referring domains.
- **Paid:** spend, CPC, cost/qualified lead, cost/consultation, call quality,
  landing-page conversion, estimate value.
- **Social:** reach/engagement on project content, profile/site actions, video
  completion, retargeting audience growth, partner engagement.
- **Business:** total/qualified inquiries, consultations, estimates, projects
  won, avg project value, funnel conversion rates, revenue by city/service, CAC.

## Social media operating model (19 hrs total, not unlimited)

- Primary channels: Instagram, Facebook, GBP posts. Secondary: YouTube Shorts
  (if footage supplied). Selective: Pinterest, LinkedIn (referral positioning).
- Content pillars: Project Proof, Arizona Education (Low-E, SHGC, west-facing
  homes), Owner Expertise, Trust & Reputation, Product Highlights, Local
  Presence, Partner Content, CTAs.
- Target 8-12 pieces/month, repurposed across channels; 1-2 short videos/month
  if raw footage supplied, else photo-based substitutes.
- **Out of scope:** daily community management, live DM handling, influencer
  campaigns, professional photography/video production, unlimited revisions,
  Meta ad spend/paid social (tested separately later if justified).

## Client inputs required (ongoing)

Timely access to website/analytics/GBP/ad accounts/software; accurate
licensing/insurance/product/warranty/service-area/pricing info; genuine project
photos/videos/testimonials; fast approval turnaround (slow approvals eat the
fixed monthly hour block); separately funded media budget for ads.

## Operating rules

- Deliverable counts are planning targets, not unlimited-production commitments.
  Complex dev, revision cycles, access problems, delayed approvals consume the
  same 20-hr/month block.
- New out-of-roadmap requests get substituted for lower-priority planned work,
  or quoted as additional hours — never silently absorbed.
