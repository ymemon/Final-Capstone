The work

Recovery bodywork, not a spa hour.

Both approaches are on the table. Which one a session leans on depends on what is actually restricting you, and that gets decided in the room rather than at booking.

Sports & recovery massage

For people who train, compete, or ask a lot of their body at work. Commonly booked around a training block — loosening what has tightened, working the areas limiting a movement, and getting you back to the thing you had to stop doing.

Not only for athletes. The same work suits anyone whose job holds them in one position for nine hours.

Deep tissue & orthopedic work

Slower, more specific work into the layers underneath — the long-standing tightness a light massage reaches and then leaves exactly as it was.

Worth saying plainly: deeper is not automatically better, and pressure that has you holding your breath is counterproductive. You set the pressure, and it gets adjusted whenever you say so.

Commonly booked for
  • Recurring muscle tension
  • Back pain
  • Neck & shoulder pain
  • Restricted range of motion
  • Postural strain
  • Training recovery
  • Desk-work tightness
  • Stress held in the body
