Your first visit

No mystery, no awkwardness.

Most people who have never had clinical bodywork hesitate because they do not know what happens. Here is the whole thing.

We talk first

A few minutes on what hurts, when it started, what makes it worse, and what you need to get back to doing. You stay dressed for this part.

Then we work

Hands-on treatment for the rest of the session, at a pressure you choose. You are draped throughout and can change your mind about anything at any point.

You leave with a plan

What was found, what to do between sessions, and an honest answer on whether you need to come back at all.

About the studio

One therapist. The same hands every visit.

Rebound Body Studio exists on a simple belief: people in pain deserve better options than being handed a rolling schedule of whoever is free. You see the same person each time, so nobody re-learns your body from scratch at every appointment.

That continuity is the whole advantage of a solo studio, and it is why progress compounds here in a way it does not at a chain.

Questions

Before you book

What does a session cost?

Sixty minutes is $95 and ninety minutes is $130. That is the whole price — there is no add-on menu and nothing is upsold during your appointment.

Which length should I book?

Book sixty minutes if you can point at the problem — a shoulder, a low back, a neck. Book ninety if it is more than one area, if it is full-body, or if the pain moves around. If you book the wrong one, it gets sorted out in the session rather than held against you.

Is this a spa massage?

No. It is treatment-focused bodywork for a specific problem. It can absolutely be relaxing, but the session is built around what is restricting you rather than around an hour of ambience.

How do I book?

Online through the booking system, at any hour, or by phone on (480) 944-0494 during opening hours. Online is usually faster because you can see live availability.

What if I have an injury or a medical condition?

Mention it when you book and again at the start of the session. Some conditions change how the work is done, and a few mean it should wait or be cleared with your doctor first — you will be told which of those applies to you.
