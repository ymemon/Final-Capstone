Directions & Parking

Find the easiest way to reach and park at our Scottsdale location.

We’re conveniently located at 7373 N Scottsdale Rd., Ste. E-100, Scottsdale, AZ 85253. From Loop 101, take the Shea Blvd exit and head west. Turn left onto N Scottsdale Rd, and you’ll find our facility on the right-hand side just past E Doubletree Ranch Rd, within the Scottsdale Medical Plaza for easy access from all major routes.

Free on-site parking is available throughout the Scottsdale Medical Plaza, with plenty of spaces near our suite entrance. Accessible parking spots are also located close to the main entry for your comfort and convenience.

Valley Metro bus routes 72 and 81 serve the Scottsdale Rd and Shea Blvd area, with stops just a short walk from our clinic entrance. Both routes provide convenient access from nearby neighborhoods and major transit connections.
