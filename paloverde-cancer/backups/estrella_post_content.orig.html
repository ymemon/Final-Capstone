Directions & Parking

Find the easiest way to reach and park at our Estrella location.

We’re conveniently located at 9250 W Thomas Rd, Suite 150, Phoenix, AZ 85037. From Loop 101, take Exit 4 (Thomas Rd)and head east. Our clinic is on the right-hand side inside the West Valley Medical Center, just past 93rd Avenue. The location offers easy access from I-10, Loop 101, and surrounding West Valley neighborhoods.

Free on-site parking is available throughout the West Valley Medical Center complex. Plenty of spaces are located near our suite entrance, including accessible parking spots close to the main entry for patient convenience.

Valley Metro bus routes 29 and 41 stop near Thomas Rd and 91st Ave, just a short walk from our clinic. Both routes provide convenient access from nearby Phoenix and West Valley communities.e.
