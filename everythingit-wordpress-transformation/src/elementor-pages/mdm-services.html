Why mobile device management matters

Every device that opens business email is part of your security boundary.

A lost phone, an outdated laptop or an unmanaged personal tablet can create a route into company email, files and applications. The challenge is not simply knowing who owns each device. It is knowing whether that device is encrypted, updated, protected and permitted to access business data.

Mobile device management gives Dublin organisations a central way to enrol, configure, monitor and secure endpoints. It helps IT teams apply consistent settings, deploy approved applications and act quickly when a device is lost, replaced or no longer trusted.

  • See which devices are accessing company resources
  • Require suitable security settings before granting access
  • Separate business data from personal information where appropriate
  • Remove work access when an employee leaves or a device is lost

Microsoft Intune services Dublin

One platform for devices, applications, compliance and access.

Microsoft Intune is a cloud-based endpoint management service for securing and managing devices and applications. It can enrol and configure endpoints, deploy apps, evaluate compliance and work with Microsoft Entra Conditional Access to control access to organisational resources.

For organisations already using Microsoft 365, Intune can provide a practical route to unified endpoint management without introducing a disconnected device platform. Microsoft 365 Business Premium includes Intune Plan 1, although the correct licensing and feature set should always be checked against your tenant and requirements.

Mobile device management FAQs

Questions Dublin businesses ask about MDM and Microsoft Intune.

What is mobile device management?

Mobile device management, usually shortened to MDM, is the central administration of work devices such as phones, tablets and laptops. It can be used to enrol devices, apply settings, deploy applications, check compliance and protect access to company data.

What devices can Microsoft Intune manage?

Microsoft Intune supports management options for Windows, macOS, iOS, iPadOS and Android devices. The available controls depend on the operating system, ownership model and enrolment method.

Can MDM secure personal devices without viewing private information?

Yes, in suitable BYOD scenarios. Microsoft Intune mobile application management can protect organisational data inside supported work apps without requiring full management of the whole device. The correct approach depends on the apps, platform and level of business risk.

Does Microsoft 365 Business Premium include Intune?

Microsoft 365 Business Premium includes Microsoft Intune Plan 1. Other Microsoft suites and standalone licensing options may also include Intune. We review the tenant and licence position before recommending additional subscriptions.

Can Intune block an unsafe device from company email and files?

Intune compliance results can be used with Microsoft Entra Conditional Access to allow or block access based on defined device or application requirements. These policies must be designed and tested carefully to avoid unnecessary disruption.

How long does an MDM implementation take?

The timeline depends on the number and type of devices, ownership models, licensing, existing Microsoft 365 configuration and policy complexity. A controlled pilot is normally completed before wider rollout so user impact can be measured and corrected.

Do you provide MDM support outside Dublin?

Yes. Everything IT is based in Dublin and supports approved service locations in Cork, Galway, Limerick and Waterford. Most design, deployment and ongoing management can be delivered remotely, with planned on-site assistance where required.
