What cybersecurity means for your organisation

Protect the business without creating unnecessary complexity.

Cybersecurity is not one product or one firewall. Everything IT combines the Huntress Managed Security Platform with coordinated protection for identities, devices, email, cloud services, networks, data and recovery processes. Each control should reduce a clearly understood risk.

Everything IT helps organisations in Dublin and across Ireland assess their current exposure, strengthen weak controls and build a practical security roadmap. The aim is to reduce the likelihood and impact of ransomware, phishing, account compromise, unauthorised access, insider risk and data loss.

Our work is shaped around your users, systems, suppliers, regulatory requirements and operational priorities. That means security decisions are documented, proportionate and connected to real business outcomes.

Security controls should work together.

Common questions

Cybersecurity services for Irish businesses.

Clear answers about how an engagement begins and what the work can include.

Is Everything IT a Huntress partner in Ireland?

Yes. Everything IT works with Huntress to provide Irish organisations with local access to the Huntress Managed Security Platform, implementation expertise and ongoing managed support.

Which Huntress services can Everything IT provide?

Everything IT provides Huntress Managed EDR for endpoints, Managed ITDR for Microsoft 365 identities and Managed SIEM for security visibility, investigation and compliance support.

Is Huntress monitored around the clock?

Yes. The Huntress Security Operations Centre operates 24/7. Its threat analysts investigate suspicious activity, support containment and escalate high-severity incidents to Everything IT.

How quickly can Huntress be deployed?

Deployment depends on the environment, but the official Everything IT case study records hundreds of Huntress agents being deployed in approximately ten minutes.

What cybersecurity services does Everything IT provide?

Microsoft 365 security, identity and access management, endpoint protection, email security, network controls, vulnerability reduction, incident response planning, backup and recovery alignment, user awareness and ISO 27001 support.

Do you only provide cybersecurity services in Dublin?

No. Everything IT is based in Dublin and supports organisations across Ireland, including multi-site and hybrid working environments.

Can you secure our Microsoft 365 environment?

Yes. We can strengthen MFA, Conditional Access, administrative privileges, email protection, sharing controls and device access.

Can cybersecurity be combined with managed IT services?

Yes. Security can be coordinated with monitoring, patching, Microsoft 365, devices, backups and response through one provider.

How does a cybersecurity engagement begin?

We begin by understanding your environment, risks, priorities and current controls, then produce a practical roadmap showing what should be addressed first and why.
